Waggle: Bee Puzzles · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Summary

Waggle does not collect, store on servers, or share any personal data. The games work fully offline.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (the level you have reached in Queen Bees and in Pollen Run) is saved only on your device, so that you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. Android may include it in your personal device backup, according to your own backup settings.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, files or other personal information.

Advertising and purchases

The current version shows no third-party ads and offers no in-app purchases. If they are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released, describing which providers are used and what data they process.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play page.
